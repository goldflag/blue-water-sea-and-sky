# Third-party notices: naval-clouds

The clouds are a port of `@takram/three-clouds` (packages/clouds of
https://github.com/takram-design-engineering/three-geospatial): its GLSL shaders (the cloud march, the Beer shadow
maps and their temporal pass, the temporal upscaling, the haze, the light shafts' shadow length, the procedural
turbulence) ported to WGSL in `shaders/`, and its cloud layers, quality presets and cascaded shadow maps ported to
Rust in `src/`. Two of its shipped textures are embedded as lossless PNGs (converted from the
originals, texel for texel): `assets/shape.png` (from `shape.bin`) and the shared
`crates/naval-sim/src/clouds/local_weather.png`. The renderer-free layer profiles live beside that shared texture,
with their MIT notice; this crate re-exports them. (Its `shape_detail.bin` was
embedded until 2026-10-04; the detail noise is now this crate's own, generated at startup by
`shaders/detail_noise.wgsl`.)

`assets/blue_noise.png` is this crate's own (generated by `examples/blue_noise.rs`). takram ships NVIDIA's
spatiotemporal blue noise (STBN) texture, whose licence allows non-commercial use only; it is not used or shipped
here.

The atmosphere the clouds are lit through (Bruneton's precomputed scattering as takram modified it) is
`naval-sky`'s; see its own notices.
